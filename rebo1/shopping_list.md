# 🛒 DIY Robot Parts & Scavenging Options

## 1. Wheels — plastic water bottles
- Cut 2L PET bottle into disc wheels (~8-12 cm dia) — tire + rim from same plastic.
- Bottle caps as spinner wheels (cap + bottle as tire) — best DIY option.
- Cut sports/juice bottles into discs.
- Cap as hub, bottle wall as tire; add straw/axle.

## 2. Motors (you have 4 from off-brand controllers; add more)
- Printer NEMA 17 stepper motors (inkjet/laser printer) — ~$10 new / free scavenged.
- CD/DVD drive pager motors (~3V).
- Electric toothbrush / hair clipper / shaver motor (~3-6V).
- Toy car motors, RC car motors, electric screwdriver motors.
- Fan motors / computer PSU fans (12V) with step-down.

## 3. Motor Controller / Driver (H-bridge) — MANY OPTIONS
- L298N Dual H-Bridge module (~$3) — most common, best starting point.
- TB6612FNG module (~$2-4) — better, less heat.
- L293D IC (DIP/SOIC) — classic.
- DRV8833 dual H-bridge (~$2-3).
- Salvage: RC car ESCs (2x) — ESC is basically an H-bridge, great free option.

## 4. Battery
- 4x AA NiMH rechargeable (~$10-15) — common, 5-6V.
- 2x LiPo 7.4V (~$8-12) + 5V regulator for Pi.
- USB power bank (5V) — scavenged/free.
- 18650 Li-ion packs (~$5-8) + holder.

## 5. SD Card / Storage (29.5 GB target)
- MicroSD 32GB SanDisk Ultra / Samsung EVO / Kingston (~$6-8).
- Larger: SanDisk 64GB/128GB, Samsung BAR, Kingston Canvas.
- SSD via USB adapter for AI model storage.

## 6. Sensors
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic (~$2-3) — most common.
- JSN-SR04T — cheaper variant.
- Sharp GP2Y0A21 IR distance (~$2-4).
- VL53L0X Time-of-Flight I2C (~$3-5).
- DIY: limit switches (reed+magnet), IR break sensor.

## 7. Camera
- Official Pi Camera Module v2 (~$25).
- USB webcam Logitech C270/C310 (~$10-15).
- Arducam/IMX219 module.

## 8. Other essentials
- Breadboard + dupont jumper wires (from any kit).
- Resistors/caps for voltage divider + decoupling.
- SG90 servo (~$3) — steering / wing flap.
- A4988 stepper driver (~$3-5) + stepper motor.
- Arcade/push button for kill switch.

## 9. Flying mechanism
- SG90 servo — wing flap / tilt control.
- Shape-memory wire / fishing line + arm.

## 10. Where to scavenge / buy
- eBay, Amazon, Facebook Marketplace, thrift stores, garage sales, electronics recyclers.
- Printers, RC cars, toys, electronics kits, shavers/toothbrushes, fans.
